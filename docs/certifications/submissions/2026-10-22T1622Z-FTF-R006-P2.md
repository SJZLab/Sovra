## What was built
- `packages/integrations/argilla/review_mapping.py` — stdlib REST adapter for the pinned Argilla v2.8.0
  dataset / record / response surface over P1a's `ArgillaTransport` (client.py untouched). Full-settings
  verification of an adopted dataset (field + `decision` accept/reject + `rationale`), strict parsers,
  bounded pages and bulk sizes, 65,536-char record cap (refused, never truncated).
- `apps/api/app/reviews/review_queue_store.py` — `create_review_queue` (ordered refusals writing nothing:
  `sealed_purpose_refused` as an allowlist, `grant_changed` live, `dataset_already_split`,
  `quality_not_clear`, `queue_too_large`, plus `record_too_large_for_review`, `source_changed`,
  `no_records`), idempotent/resumable push with verified adoption of pre-existing records;
  `submit_review_response` (reviewer's own OpenBao-resolved key, `expected_version` under
  `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` on the item row, authorization re-checked under the lock, read-back by the
  provisioning key, `response_sha256`, content-free audit); `reconcile_review_queue` (authenticated
  polling; per-candidate re-verification under the item lock; replaced/missing/edited record, unmapped or
  owner response, changed/draft/discarded/missing response → sticky drift; an untracked valid response by
  an active reviewer is reported `response_pending_adoption`, never recorded, never drift).
- Migration `0036_review_queues` (down_revision 0034; PR #934 holds 0035 — whichever merges second
  rechains): `review_queues` / `review_items` / `review_responses`, ENABLE+FORCE RLS, `tenant_isolation`,
  SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE only, composite FK to `uq_datasets_quality_lineage`, UQ (queue_id, record_id),
  UQ (item_id, identity_id), sealed-purpose CHECK, hash/UUID CHECKs, three update-guard triggers
  (frozen columns, `building→ready` only, sticky drift, `row_version` +1).
- Tests: `tests/tasks/test_ftf_r006_p2.py` (42 functions; real-socket `FakeArgillaServer` extended with
  the dataset/record/response surface and real `X-Argilla-Api-Key` auth, SQLite standing in for Postgres,
  real JSONL through the real importer), `tests/integration/test_argilla_review_queue.py` (real PG16 as
  `sovra_app`, real OpenBao in CI), CI: one gate step + job `argilla-review-queue-integration` in
  `ci-verdict` needs (ci.yml change is purely additive).
