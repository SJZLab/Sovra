## What changed (production: `apps/web/components/dataset-factory/dataset_issue_resolution_ui.tsx`)
1. **One response-binding function** `decisionMatchesRequest` (class-closing ruling). On a 2xx it compares every field sent with the decision returned: report id, issue id, action, before-evidence, trimmed reason, sorted positions (exact list), successor; and checks the server-derived fields against the action: `engine_decision` is `waived` exactly for `waive`; `after_evidence_sha256` is present exactly when the action is not `waive`; `resolution_version_after` equals the sent `expected_resolution_version + 1`; the top-level `resolution_version` equals the same. Any mismatch is "resolution response does not match the request", never "Decision recorded". The decision is taken as `items[0]` of a single-item response, so the issue check is load-bearing.
2. **`parseIssue` binds each nested resolution** to its issue (`issue_id`), its report (`quality_report_id` = `report_id`) and its state (`engine_decision` = `state`); `parseResolution` additionally requires a non-blank `actor_id` and a non-blank (trimmed) `reason`.
3. **Submit during a reload**: `canSubmit` now includes `!loading` (a submit can no longer supersede a reload and leave "Loading..." stuck/Load disabled after a 409), and "Load more issues" is disabled while a decision is submitting.
4. Simplifications that removed equivalent mutants: the redundant first `stale()` check in `handleSubmit`, the redundant `setDraft(EMPTY_DRAFT)` after success (the form closes and `review` resets the draft), and a single `sentPositions` helper (toggling never duplicates a position).
