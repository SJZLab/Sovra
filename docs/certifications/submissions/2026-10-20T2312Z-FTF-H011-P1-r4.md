## Point 2: corrected cp1256 disclosure (Control-plane ruling)

The round-3 submission said "finalize refuses a non-UTF-8 text/csv, so the contract cannot be met".
That is inaccurate: finalize only sniffs the first 64 KiB, decoding as UTF-8, so the real behaviour
depends on where the first non-ASCII byte falls. The gap is in certified FTF-0501 finalize, not in
P1, and is queued as FTF-0501-F2. P1's cp1256 fixture (cp1256 `;` CSV with an Arabic header on
row 3) is WAIVED by the Control plane; no >64 KiB-preamble workaround is added. FTF-H011-P4 re-adds
the cp1256 journey once FTF-0501-F2 is certified.
