Design: `authorize_external_provider` composes FTF-0901 `effective_profile`/`enforce_effect` (profile ordering is not re-implemented). Training maps to the `model` surface, inference to `serving`. `zero_egress`/`air_gapped` deny every managed route; `standard` needs a `ProviderApproval` for the exact provider and purpose with approver, account, region, retention and that purpose's terms. A denial raises `ExternalProviderDeniedError` (stable `code`, `reason` = profile/surface/purpose/code). With an `AuditContext` it is also written through the certified FTF-0104 `write_audit_event` (details exactly profile/surface/purpose/code).
