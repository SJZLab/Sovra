## Design

`grade_exact_match(candidate_value, approved_values, *, case_policy, whitespace_policy)`:
full-string `==` comparison only, after applying the caller's **explicit, required (no
default)** `case_policy` (`case_sensitive`/`case_insensitive`) and `whitespace_policy`
(`exact`/`strip`/`collapse`) to both sides. `approved_values` is a non-empty sequence of
canonical acceptable strings — any one matching passes; the matched value is reported.
