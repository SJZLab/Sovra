## Disclosed choices and gaps
- Locator is `line:<start physical line>`; the frozen locator grammar cannot carry both ordinal and line.
  The ordinal is the candidate's `record_index` (counts rejected rows, not blank lines).
- The fatal line is returned as `CsvImportOutcome.fatal_locator` because `ImportReport` has no field for it.
- `header_row` counts non-blank records before the header; they are an owner-declared preamble, skipped.
- A physical line over `2*max_record_bytes+2` characters is fatal `field_too_large` (memory bound).
- **Not built: `tests/integration/test_ftf_0504_csv_import_postgres_s3.py` and job
  `csv-import-integration`.** No import route or storage-backed caller exists for the importer, same
  as the sibling importers. Recorded as BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY, not skipped. Needs a Control-plane ruling.
- No live caller: `proving_symbol` blank, product-true stays 47/47.
