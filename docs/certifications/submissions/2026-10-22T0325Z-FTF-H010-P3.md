## Disclosed
- UNWIRED dispatcher (FTF-0801/H016), same as P2.
- `factory_trained` parents are refused `base_not_validated`: no factory-run entity exists to prove them.
- `model.safetensors.index.json` is allow-listed and hashed but not interpreted.
- `private_s3` / `huggingface` artifacts end `blocked_external metadata_files_unavailable` (no multi-file resolver
  manifest yet); loading weights (vLLM/GPU) and trainer compatibility stay BLOCKED_EXTERNAL (FTF-1003/1004,
  FTF-R003).
- Every shard is hashed three times (FTF-0306 digest, manifest re-resolve, header pass), a cost chosen over a
  weaker binding.
- No spec-matrix entry is added or flipped: P3 is a child slice with no matrix key (P3a precedent); the parent
  FTF-H010 stays NONE/ABSENT until P4 lands.
