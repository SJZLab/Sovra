## What shipped

- Migration `0027_model_import` (chained onto `0026_dataset_quality_issues`, the real `main`
  head at claim time — `0026_dataset_quality_issues`/PR #639 was still open; rebase if it merges
  first and this round is still open):
  - `model_versions` gains 8 new §6.17 columns: `origin` (NOT NULL, a finer classification than
    the existing `import_kind`), `lineage_completeness`, `adapter_only`, `parent_model_version_id`
    (self-referential composite FK, RESTRICT), `external_provider_model_ref`,
    `observed_deployment_fingerprint`, `tokenizer_revision`, `chat_template_sha256`,
    `serving_config_sha256`. Backfilled from `import_kind`/`endpoint_reference` for pre-existing
    rows. Two new structural CHECKs: `external_endpoint` requires no local artifact/digest and a
    real `external_provider_model_ref`; `imported_adapter` requires `adapter_only` and a real
    `base_model_revision`. Two new format CHECKs (`chat_template_sha256`/`serving_config_sha256`
    `~ '^[0-9a-f]{64}$'` when not NULL).
  - New `model_import_jobs`: ENABLE+FORCE RLS, `tenant_isolation` policy, `SELECT, INSERT` plus a
    column-limited `UPDATE (state, failure_code, updated_at)`, never DELETE. `state` enum
    `queued→validating→{failed,succeeded,blocked_external}`; `failure_code` present iff
    `state in (failed, blocked_external)`.
- **Critical regression caught and fixed before submission** (self-attack, not from a review
  round): `model_version_schema.py`'s `ModelVersion` ORM class did not declare the 8 new columns
  at all. The new NOT NULL `origin` column and the two new structural CHECKs would have broken
  every existing call to `create_model_version` (FTF-1001, already CERTIFIED) the moment this
  migration applied — confirmed directly against a real local Postgres instance before the fix
  existed. Fixed with backward-compatible defaulting entirely inside `ModelVersion.__init__`
  (origin/lineage_completeness/adapter_only derived from `import_kind`;
  `external_provider_model_ref` copied from `endpoint_reference` for an `endpoint` import); an
  explicit value always wins over a derived one. FTF-1001's own API/request contract is
  completely unchanged — `model_version_crud.py` was not touched at all.
- `apps/api/app/model_versions/model_import_schema.py` + `model_import_crud.py` +
  `model_import_routes.py`: `POST`/`GET`/`GET` on
  `.../organizations/{org}/projects/{project}/model-imports[/{id}]`, mounted in `main.py`. POST
  validates shape only and enqueues at `state=queued` — it never claims success synchronously
  (Law 17) and nothing in this slice can ever move a job past `queued` (no worker exists yet;
  FTF-H010's own later job, disclosed in the routes module's own docstring).
- Contract frozen in a new `packages/contracts/openapi/10-interface.yaml` (additive phase file;
  does not touch `03`/`05`-interface.yaml or FTF-1001's own synchronous `model-versions` surface).
- CI (additive only): gate step for `tests/tasks/test_ftf_h010_p1.py`, new
  `model-import-integration` job (real Postgres 16), its `ci-verdict` needs entry.
- Not touched: `model_version_crud.py`, `model_version_routes.py`'s own request/response shape,
  `03`/`05`-interface.yaml, any other migration.

  more conservative, provably-true classification was chosen over an unproven one (Law 1).
