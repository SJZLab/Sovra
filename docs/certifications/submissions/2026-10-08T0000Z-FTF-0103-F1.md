## Change
`apps/api/app/auth/rbac_middleware.py`: new `MAX_JSON_BODY_BYTES = 33554432` (32 MiB, the decided cap). `_read_json_body` first calls `_read_capped_body`: a declared Content-Length over the cap -> 413 `{"detail":"request_too_large"}` without reading; otherwise the body is streamed and stops at the cap with the same 413 (covers chunked bodies with no Content-Length). The bytes are stored in Starlette's own body cache so the route handler reads them as before. Lower route-level caps still apply. No new dependency. One additive CI step (`FTF-0103-F1 negative-test proof`) in the existing `gate` job's step list.
